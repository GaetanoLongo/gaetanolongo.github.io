The course was taught by Prof. Nucci in academic year 2014/2015 ([programme](http://www.ingegneriarchitettura.unibo.it/it/corsi/insegnamenti/insegnamento/2014/376368)).

The folder contains:

- notes written by Marco Cavallina (.doc);
- computer lab exercises;
- solved exercises for the written exam;
- Prof. Giorgi's book "Esercitazioni di impianti elettrici", useful because it closely follows the exercises done in class;
- a spreadsheet for the written exam, made by me in Excel (or rather OpenOffice).

The spreadsheet quickly computes basic quantities, network parameters and star/delta/star transformations. I tried to make it reasonably clear.
