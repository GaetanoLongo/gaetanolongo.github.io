### HW Engineer · Montrade SpA / Almasens
<p class="cv-meta">Aug 2024 – present</p>

Almasens is the R&D division of Montrade SpA, which makes machinery for the tobacco industry. The sensors needed for in-line quality assurance are developed in-house. My role is to design these sensors and their electronics, and to run feasibility studies with a focus on system-level integration.

- Design of an electrovalve driver to improve switching speed, from requirements to final board design and validation.
- Development of a near-infrared spectroscopy system for non-contact measurement of moisture and nicotine content, including optical design, electronic hardware and measurement methodology.
- Feasibility study for upgrading a pulsed power source for cold plasma generation (up to 2 kW peak) using magnetic compression.

</div>

### HW team leader · OCEM Power Electronics
<p class="cv-meta">May 2017 – Aug 2024</p>

OCEM Power Electronics specialises in customised power converters. From July 2021 I was Hardware Team Leader, coordinating and supporting a team of five engineers (two PhD candidates and three M.Sc. graduates). Over seven years I:

- took part in tenders: requirement analysis, feasibility assessment, technical proposals and cost evaluation;
- led and supported the design of power converters: simulation, component selection and validation, power stages, protection circuits, auxiliary and measurement systems, safety circuits;
- ran testing and commissioning, in-house and at customer sites, including test procedures and acceptance protocols;
- troubleshot complex power converters and repaired electronic boards;
- worked with the mechanical, control and purchasing departments;
- worked with suppliers and wrote specifications for custom components such as transformers, semiconductor assemblies and supercapacitor banks.

</div>

### Selected projects as technical lead

- **APS main dipole converters**: `+1500 V / 500 A` and `+1000 V / 220 A`. Two converters for the magnet system of the Advanced Photon Source synchrotron (Argonne, USA), with 10 ppm accuracy and current stability over one week. Water-cooled.
- **GSI magnet test stand**: `±400 V / 4000 A`. Four-quadrant converter with active front end for high-current magnet testing at the GSI accelerator facility (Germany), 100 ppm accuracy and stability, with a crowbar for magnet discharge. Water-cooled.
- **Tokamak Energy PF converter**: `±500 V / 3800 A`. Four-quadrant converter with supercapacitor energy storage for an experimental high-temperature superconducting magnet (UK), with a crowbar discharging onto silicon carbide varistors. Water-cooled.
