---
title: "Dunking biscuits"
description: "Biscuits made with baker's ammonia, from a dough that rests overnight."
date: 2026-11-16
cook: "15 min"
tags: ["desserts", "biscuits"]
ingredients:
  - { q: "430 g (15 oz)", i: "Flour" }
  - { q: "3", i: "Eggs" }
  - { q: "50 g (1¾ oz)", i: "Neutral vegetable oil" }
  - { q: "170 g (6 oz)", i: "Sugar" }
  - { q: "7 g (¼ oz)", i: "Baker's ammonia (ammonium carbonate)" }
  - { q: "½ tsp", i: "Vanilla extract (optional)" }
  - { q: "as needed", i: "Granulated sugar, for rolling" }
---

1. Beat the eggs with the sugar for a few minutes, then add the oil and the sifted flour mixed with the baker's ammonia. Work the dough well until everything is combined.
2. Cover the bowl with a tea towel and let the dough rest overnight (or longer).
3. Shape the dough into finger-wide ropes, cut them into 7-8 cm (about 3 in) pieces, roll them in granulated sugar and place them well apart on a baking tray lined with parchment paper: they spread as they bake.
4. Bake at 180 °C (355 °F) for about 15 minutes. Wedge a spoon in the oven door to let the steam out. You will smell strong ammonia while they bake: that is normal, and nothing of it remains in the biscuits.

**Note**

- A faster way: roll the dough out into a rectangle about 1-1.5 cm (½ in) thick, sprinkle with granulated sugar, turn it over and dust the other side with more sugar. Cut strips 1-1.5 cm (½ in) wide with a pizza wheel and place them on the tray.

> Tasty!
