1. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm milk with the sugar and let it rise for about half an hour.
2. Put the ingredients from the eggs to the pecorino romano into a bowl, in the order listed, and finally the yeast dissolved in the milk. Mix. The liquid ingredients with the salt should only come into contact with the yeast at the moment of mixing.
3. Add the fresh pecorino in small pieces (optional).
4. Grease the panettone-style mould, pour in the dough and place the mould on the lowest rack of the oven. Do not move the mould out of the oven again, and let it rise for 2 to 4 hours.
5. Baking: set the oven to 50 °C (120 °F) and turn on the grill. Once it reaches 50 °C (120 °F), raise the temperature to 170-180 °C (340-355 °F) and switch to conventional heat, top and bottom. When it reaches baking temperature, bake for another hour.
