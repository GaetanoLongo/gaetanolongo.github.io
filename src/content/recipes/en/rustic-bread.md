1. Mix flour, water and licoli until no dry flour is left. Rest for 30 minutes.
2. Add the salt and fold the dough a few times to work it in.
3. Let it rise, folding every 30 minutes for the first two hours, until about 50% bigger.
4. Shape the loaf and rest it in a floured basket.
5. Bake in a preheated, covered pot: 20 minutes covered, then 25 minutes uncovered until deep golden.
