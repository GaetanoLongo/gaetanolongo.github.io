Final recipe for a 750 g (26 oz) panettone, with a flour blend equivalent to a flour with 15% protein. **Always use the same proportion for all the flour in the recipe**: 55.6% flour with 16.6% protein and 44.4% flour with 13% protein, 330 g (11.6 oz) in total. For two panettoni double all the quantities (660 g / 23.3 oz of flour: 366 g / 12.9 oz + 294 g / 10.4 oz).

1. **Lievitino.** Make the lievitino with the ingredients listed. Correct sign: after 30-40 min at 26-28 °C (79-82 °F) it should be puffy, full of holes, not collapsed.
2. **First dough.** In a Kenwood stand mixer with the J hook:
   - speed 1: flour, water and lievitino (3-4 min); add the water in small amounts;
   - speed 1-1.5: the egg, in a thin stream;
   - speed 1.5-2: the sugar;
   - speed 2: the butter, in 3-4 additions.

   The dough is ready when it comes away completely from the bowl, wraps around the hook, has a smooth glossy surface and stretches into an elastic membrane (not yet very thin). Dough temperature: 25-26 °C (77-79 °F). Cover with cling film: it should double or triple, in about 2 h at 25 °C (77 °F).
3. **Second dough.** In the Kenwood stand mixer with the J hook.
   - First dough and flour: speed 1, then 1.5 and 2 (to build the strength again, "ri-incordare");
   - sugar: speed 1.5-2;
   - egg and yolk in a thin stream: speed 1-1.5;
   - butter in several additions: speed 2;
   - citrus paste: speed 1;
   - salt: speed 1;
   - raisins, candied peel and/or chocolate chips (100-150 g / 3.5-5.3 oz in total, as you prefer): speed 1, only to mix them in. This is the last step of the last mixing.

   The dough is ready when it forms a very thin, transparent membrane, is shiny and silky, stays on the hook "like a flag" and lifts without tearing. Final temperature: 26-27 °C (79-81 °F).
4. **Bulk rise, dividing, proofing.** Cover for 20 min, then do two folds in the bowl. Do the last fold on a board greased with butter, otherwise the dough tears. Then ball it up (pirlatura) and put the dough in the mould to rise for 5-6 h, at 26-28 °C (79-82 °F), until it is 1-2 cm (½-¾ in) from the rim.
5. **Baking.** 160 °C (320 °F) conventional, for 45-50 min, until 94-95 °C (201-203 °F) at the core. Turn the panettone upside down immediately to cool (8-10 h).

## Citrus paste in brief

- It is made with 300 g (10.6 oz) of whole citrus fruit (pulp and peel) and 150 g (5.3 oz) of sugar, 450 g (15.9 oz) in all, which are cooked and reduced: the weight usually drops to about 250-300 g (9-10.6 oz).
- In the second dough, 40 g (1.4 oz) of citrus paste replace the honey, citrus zest and vanilla of the traditional flavouring.
- Instead of the citrus paste you can use a similar amount of jam, if it is quite thick.
- *Typical method, not given in the source document:* wash the citrus well and cut it into pieces, removing the seeds; blanch it, even several times changing the water, to soften the bitterness; blend it with the sugar and cook the mixture until it becomes a thick paste; let it cool and keep it in a jar in the fridge.
