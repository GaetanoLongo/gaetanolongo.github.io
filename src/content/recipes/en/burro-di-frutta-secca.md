1. Roast the nuts in the oven for about 30 minutes at 130 °C (265 °F).
2. Taste them: they should be dry, not damp.
3. Let them cool completely, then blend in a food processor until liquid. At first they will look like a powder: don't get discouraged, keep going.
4. Stop the processor every so often to let the blades cool down.
5. When it is ready, pour into a glass jar and close the lid only after a while, once it has reached room temperature.

It works with hazelnuts, walnuts, almonds or any other nut.

**Notes**

- You need at least 500 g (1.1 lb) of nuts, so that the food processor bowl is well filled. A food processor works better than a jug blender.
- It usually takes 5-15 minutes, scraping down the sides now and then. Almonds are drier and take longer: if it won't turn creamy, a spoonful of neutral oil helps.
- A pinch of salt makes a big difference.
- For a milder taste, rub hazelnuts in a tea towel while still warm to remove the skins.
- It thickens as it cools. If oil separates on top, that is normal: just stir it.
- Store in the fridge: walnut butter in particular goes rancid quickly.
