  - { q: "500 g (1.1 lb)", i: "Manitoba flour (strong bread flour)" }
  - { q: "2", i: "Eggs" }
  - { q: "180 ml (6 fl oz)", i: "Milk" }
  - { q: "1 cube", i: "Fresh yeast (25 g / 0.9 oz)" }
  - { q: "80 g (2¾ oz)", i: "Butter" }
  - { q: "1 tbsp", i: "Sugar" }
  - { q: "1 tsp", i: "Salt" }

**For a sweet preparation:** add 2 tablespoons of sugar (about 30 g / 1 oz) to the dough and leave out the salt.

1. Melt the butter in a bain-marie or in the microwave.
2. Warm the milk slightly (it should be lukewarm, not hot) and dissolve the yeast in it together with 1 tablespoon of sugar.
3. Put the flour in a bowl and add the eggs. For the sweet version, add the 2 tablespoons of sugar here.
4. Mix, then add the melted butter.
5. Mix again, then add the mixture of yeast, milk and sugar.
6. Knead and finally add the salt (leave it out for the sweet version).
7. Transfer everything to a lightly floured work surface and keep kneading by hand until the dough is smooth and even.
8. Shape it into a ball and let it rise for at least 1 hour, or until doubled in volume, in a warm place away from drafts (a closed, switched-off oven is perfect).
9. At this point the pan brioche is ready to be used in your preparation, with the filling you prefer.
10. To bake it, use the same settings as version 1: oven at 180 °C (355 °F) for about 45 minutes.
