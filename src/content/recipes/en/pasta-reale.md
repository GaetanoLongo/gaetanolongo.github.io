---
title: "Pasta reale (spugnette)"
description: "Small cubes of egg, semolina and Parmesan, cooked in a bag and then in meat broth."
date: 2026-11-16
cook: "about 1 hour"
tags: ["first courses", "broth"]
ingredients:
  - { q: "1", i: "Egg" }
  - { q: "1 tbsp", i: "Fine semolina (semolino)" }
  - { q: "1 tbsp", i: "Parmesan" }
  - { q: "to taste", i: "Salt, pepper and nutmeg" }
  - { q: "optional", i: "Lemon zest" }
---

1. Mix until you have a creamy mixture.
2. Put it in a suitable bag, close it and boil it in stock-cube broth for about 1 hour.
3. Cut into small cubes and cook in meat broth.

They can be frozen.
