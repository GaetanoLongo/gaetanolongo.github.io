1. Put the eggs in a food processor and beat them with the sugar until frothy. Add the oil, the water, the rum (optional), the flour and finally the baking powder. If you like, add floured raisins too.
2. Butter a ring mould and pour in ¾ of the batter.
3. Mix the 2 tablespoons of unsweetened cocoa into the remaining batter, then pour it into the mould on top of the white batter.
4. Draw concentric circles with a knife to create the marbled effect.
5. Bake in a hot conventional oven (no fan) at 180 °C (355 °F) for about 40 minutes. Check with the toothpick test.

**Tips**

- The batter will end up quite runny: do not add more flour. It is meant to be like that, and it is the secret of its softness.
- You can optionally add grated lemon zest, rum, chocolate chips or raisins.
- If you use milk instead of water, the result is not the same.
